## 📌 Overview

Welcome to the **Networking Fundamentals** section of my Networking & Cybersecurity Portfolio.

This section documents my understanding of core computer networking concepts developed through CCNA studies, technical documentation, practical exercises, and hands-on learning.

It covers the fundamental technologies and concepts that form the foundation of modern computer networks, including network architecture, communication models, networking devices, IP addressing, subnetting, protocols, and troubleshooting.

My objective is to strengthen my networking foundation and apply this knowledge to real-world networking environments and cybersecurity operations.
